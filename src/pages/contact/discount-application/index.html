Client Input Form — Discount Application

We enjoy and value working with organisations that provide charitable support and/or voluntary services. To show this we offer a 10% discount to any registered charity or organisation that works to provide a service for a good cause. We also extend this discount to all new businesses less than a year old.

Terms & Conditions
  • This is a discretionary discount and is subject to approval.
  • To benefit, your organisation must be one of the following:
    • A registered charity — proof of charitable status must be supplied.
    • A business less than one year old — proof of company start date must be supplied.
    • An organisation that provides support/services to the community, the environment, or animal welfare.
  • The discount is applied to the total cost and taken off the final payment.
  • This application may be submitted at any time prior to final payment being received, but not after.
