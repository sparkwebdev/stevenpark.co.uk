Archived projects from earlier phases of practice and development.

Navigator Partnership

Website

Built with: WordPress

Design by Michelle Haswell Design

A responsive WordPress website for a Glasgow-based marketing services partner working across web, digital and creative. Modern with a duotone-like colour scheme; interface elements that break out of a structured grid layout.

SooUK

E-commerce Website

Built with: WordPress & WooCommerce

Design by David Park

A contemporary online design store based near Edinburgh. The site showcases a mutual love of beautiful product design, interiors and architecture. Modern and contemporary with a minimal black & white colour scheme, allowing bold product and interior photos to stand out.

The Pet Business

E-Commerce Website

Built with: Shopify

A retailer, wholesaler and distributor of high quality pet products with a focus on quality, ethical, innovative products. Most products sourced from American and Canadian companies. Fun and warm visual design; soft lily pink and purple colour scheme.

Variprint

E-commerce Website

Built with: WordPress & WooCommerce

A bold, modern and mobile-responsive design for a print and design company based in Paisley, near Glasgow. VariPrint differentiates themselves by focussing on quality, being eco-conscious and not being afraid to pull an all-nighter! Dark overall with bright splashes of brand red; fairly complex grid-based layout.

Forth Valley Art Beat

Mobile App and Website

Built with: Ionic, React & WordPress

A cross-platform mobile app and responsive WordPress website for the 'Open Studios Weekend' and 'Art Cycle' events. One of the largest cultural events in the Forth Valley area, the app helps visitors find venues, discover artists and get directions. Website: GTmetrix Grade A, 99/90.
