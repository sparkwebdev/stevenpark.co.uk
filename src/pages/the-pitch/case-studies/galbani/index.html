Galbani

Type: Website

Built using: WordPress

A tasty website for Italy's No.1 cheese brand, featuring a recipe finder with exclusive recipes from chefs and food bloggers.

About Galbani

Dating back to 1882, when father and son Davide & Egidio Galbani first made cheese in the Alpine valley village of Ballabio, Galbani is Italy's favourite cheese brand — selling more than 300 products in 50 countries.

Recipe Finder & Content

For the site, Galbani teamed up with several chefs and food bloggers to create a range of exclusive recipes using Galbani products — e.g. Lamb Cutlet, Sweet Potato & Mozzarella Tray Bake; Roasted Pepper, Sausage and Mozzarella Bake; Chocolate Ice Cream with Mascarpone.

Recipes can be searched and filtered by style, course and cooking time. Recipes are dynamically linked to their products and author, allowing users to browse content types with ease and share to social media.

Technology

Built using WordPress with a mobile-friendly responsive design. WordPress Custom Fields store the various product/recipe content types; a custom search implementation powers the recipe finder.

Visual Design

Galbani's blue brand colour is prominent throughout; lots of food photography bring the products and recipes to life.
